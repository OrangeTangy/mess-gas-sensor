Connect brain needs Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop; live readings show only on the computer the brain is plugged into. Otherwise this page shows readings published about every 6 minutes by a computer running the bridge; GitHub Pages can add up to 10 minutes of caching, so expect readings to be 5–20 minutes old. Gaps mean the sensor or laptop was offline; no values are filled in.
